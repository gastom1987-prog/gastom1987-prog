# WareRushAI

A practical inventory workflow for recommerce and overstock merchants.

Small businesses handling overstock and returned goods often need to identify products, prepare listings, keep stock organized, and get items ready for online sales. WareRushAI is being built to bring that work together in one workflow. AI helps with product identification and suggestions, while people review and approve important decisions.

## What it does

WareRushAI is designed to help merchants:

- Capture products by barcode or photo
- Prepare product information and suggest categories and prices
- Manage stock and keep inventory organized
- Connect products with online sales channels

Shopify is the current webshop integration. Other integrations and capabilities may still be in development, so their status should be checked before being treated as generally available.
